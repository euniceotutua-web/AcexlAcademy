How it works

Four steps to a stronger semester

1

Reach out

Call, email or send the form to tell us about your student and what you are hoping to change.

2

Free diagnostic

We meet your student online, find the gaps in each subject and set two or three goals together with you.

3

Pick a program

Standard, small group, bootcamp or summer academy. If cost is a concern, ask about scholarship rates right away.

4

Start learning

Sessions begin, and you receive regular progress updates from your student's tutor.

For parents

Clear goals, honest progress reports and a tutor who knows your child by name. You always know what we are working on and why.

For students

A place where it is safe to say "I do not get it yet." Get help in your hardest subjects, ask every question, and watch grades and confidence climb.

For our community

Scholarship seats, free community tutoring sessions and partnerships with schools and sponsors who share our mission.

Why we are a nonprofit

Our pay it forward model

Tuition from enrolled families does more than cover a tutor's time. It funds scholarship seats for students who need help most. Grants, sponsorships and donations grow the scholarship side even further.
