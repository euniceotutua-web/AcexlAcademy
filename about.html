About AceXL

A nonprofit built around one idea

Every student can succeed. Some just have not met the right teacher yet.

Our mission
Increase access to high quality tutoring in every subject for students, particularly students who face financial or educational barriers.
Why a foundation

AceXL Education Foundation is organized as a nonprofit education organization. That means every dollar a family pays for tutoring stays inside the foundation: it pays our tutors, builds better lessons and funds scholarship seats for students who could not otherwise afford support. There are no outside owners taking a share.

We are based in the Atlanta area and teach online, so a student anywhere in the United States can work with us.

How we teach

What makes an AceXL session different

Understanding over memorizing

We focus on the why behind each concept so skills stick past the next quiz, in math, science, reading, writing and every other subject.

A plan built around your student

We start with a diagnostic, set clear goals with you, and share progress updates you can actually read.

Aligned to what school expects

Lessons follow your student's classroom curriculum and state standards, and prepare them for class tests, GMAS, the SAT and the ACT.

Priced so no one is turned away

Sliding scholarship rates, a full scholarship track and a free community tutoring program.
