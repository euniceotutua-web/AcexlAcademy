Scholarships

No student turned away for cost

AceXL exists to increase access to high quality tutoring for students who face financial or educational barriers. Scholarships are how we keep that promise.

How it works

Pay it forward

Tuition from enrolled families pays our tutors and funds scholarship seats. For roughly every ten paying students, we can support two to three scholarship students. Grants, sponsorships and donations grow the scholarship side even further.

When you enroll with AceXL, you are investing in your own student and in another family's student at the same time.

Options

Three ways we lower the cost

Scholarship rate

Reduced tuition of $50 per month for qualifying families. Same tutors, same program, same progress updates.

Full scholarship

Awarded to students with the greatest need. The full standard or small group program at no cost, reviewed each semester.

Community tutoring

Free open help sessions any student can join, no enrollment or application required.

Applying

A simple, private application

There is no long form and no judgment. Reach out by phone, email or the contact form, tell us a little about your situation, and we will let you know what we can offer within a few days. Awards depend on family need and the funding available that semester.

Sponsors and partners

Support a student

Businesses, churches and community groups can sponsor scholarship seats, host a community tutoring session or partner with us on a summer academy. Every sponsored seat puts a tutor in front of a student who would otherwise go without.
