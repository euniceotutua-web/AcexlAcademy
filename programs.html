Questions

Frequently asked

How do online sessions work?

Sessions run live over video with a shared whiteboard. Students need a computer or tablet, a stable internet connection and a quiet spot. We send a link before each session.

How long is a session?

Standard sessions are 60 minutes. Small group sessions are 60 to 75 minutes depending on the group.

Can we switch subjects?

Yes. Your monthly plan covers whichever subject your student needs most that month. Many students use math one month and writing the next.

Is there a contract?

No long term contract. Plans are month to month and you can pause or stop with two weeks notice.

What if we cannot afford the standard rate?

Tell us. Scholarship rates start at $50 per month and full scholarships are available. See the Scholarships page.
